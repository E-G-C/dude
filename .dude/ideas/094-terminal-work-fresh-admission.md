## Managed scope notes

One outcome: safe terminal Work disposition and genuinely new admission.
Packet-growth prevention belongs in this outcome's acceptance criteria to
prevent recurrence.

- The original still-live Work owner must produce an immutable fence, effect,
  and control-evidence handoff before returning. A later explicit request may
  consume it only after current ownership, absence, retained-source, learning,
  readiness, and capacity gates pass.
- Ship uses eligible fresh admission automatically. Unsupported or incomplete
  proof produces one precise missing-proof report, not repeated requests for
  the user to supply technical facts.
- The old invocation, failure, accounting, learning, obligations, and original
  evidence remain unchanged. No dead-run revival, task reset, administrative
  close, or retrospective proof fabrication is included.
- Packet-growth prevention keeps one self-contained lossless packet and the
  exact 262,144-byte ceiling. It removes avoidable representation growth and
  checks the fully known completion footprint before accepting new capture or
  effects; unique evidence may still exceed capacity.

## Scope and authority boundaries

The staged definition covers four serial implementation slices: packet growth
and known-completion preflight; original-owner fence, retention, and disposition;
fresh same-key admission through the existing driver and lane; and owning
policy/docs, contract pins, generated parity, and installed acceptance.

This definition grants no current live capability, cleanup, restart, closure,
native consent or execution, or edits to Feature 089. Preserve all unrelated
089 source, generated files, docs, and workflow history. The reported old
post-dispatch run remains ineligible without its missing original proof.

The existing driver cannot establish escaped specialist or external-effect
settlement. Those cases refuse. Existing narrow first-Assessment release and
outside-Work manual acceptance remain distinct. Later or resolved learning is
not rewound or imported.

No supervision platform, daemon, registry, hidden live ownership store, new
workflow, cap increase, persistent compaction, or archive rewrite is included.
The current driver must supply and consume every new bounded handoff.
