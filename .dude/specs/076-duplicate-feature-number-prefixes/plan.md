## Chosen Implementation

### Shared identity inventory

In `src/skills/dude-engine/lib/feature.mjs`:

1. Remove the calls that diagnose `FEATURE_IDEA_NUMBER_DUPLICATE` and `FEATURE_PACKAGE_NUMBER_DUPLICATE`.
2. Delete the number-keyed `claims` aggregation and its `FEATURE_NUMBER_COLLISION` rejection. Do not replace it with a new registry or reservation mechanism.
3. Remove the now-unused numeric-diagnostic branches and supporting locals from `summaryIdeaDiagnostics`. Full inventory and summary selection must agree that a shared number alone is valid.
4. Retain slug-duplicate validation, `ideasBySpec` exact-path owner grouping, missing/duplicate owner diagnostics, and the owner number-and-slug consistency check.

Keep `parseIdeaIdentity`, `parseSpecIdentity`, filesystem safety, metadata validation, and fail-closed resolver behavior unchanged. The remaining checks already reject true conflicts: duplicate semantic slugs, multiple exact owners, mismatched pairs, and unowned or unsafe packages. Do not add parallel validators for those same conditions.

The inventory already sorts ideas by `numberValue` then `ideaPath`, and packages by `numberValue` then `specPath`. Keep those full-identity tie-breaks and the existing maximum-plus-one computation.

### First-definition publication

In `src/skills/dude-feature-definition/publish-first-definition.mjs`, remove the package-claim gate that rejects an existing package solely for sharing the selected idea's number. Retain the exact selected-draft check, matching number and slug in `--idea` / `--spec`, target-directory absence check, expected-missing package files, exact preimage, protected-section preservation, single appended event, and rollback-bound lint.

The target-directory and atomic expected-missing checks still reject an existing full target. The shared inventory still rejects conflicting slugs and invalid owners. No package number is allocated during definition.

`publish-first-capture.mjs` already consumes `inventory.nextNumber`, rereads the local inventory, and checks the published postimage. It needs regression coverage, not a second allocator or a production edit.

### Downstream readers

In `src/skills/dude-lightweight-execution/backlog.mjs`, remove `numberCounts`, `duplicateNumber`, and the number-only clearing of `authoritySlug` in `collectLifecycleItems`. Keep the duplicate-slug protection and existing anchors. Keep `compareLifecycle` and the existing grouping, explicit order, and dependency semantics.

### Instructions and documentation

Update the owning allocation and first-definition rules in `src/skills/dude-feature-definition/SKILL.md` and the concise lifecycle/ownership rules in `src/instructions/dude.instructions.md`. State that different slugs may share a prefix; “collision” refusals concern actual slug/path/ownership conflicts. Keep the local maximum-plus-one, no-gap-reuse, `001`-`999`, and exact-owner rules.

In `src/skills/dude-lint/SKILL.md` under `## Checks`, remove `duplicate-number` from the failing idea identities and explicitly permit otherwise valid distinct slugs to share a prefix. Retain malformed and out-of-range identity, duplicate-slug, filename/slug mismatch, unsafe-path, metadata, missing/ambiguous-owner, pair-mismatch, and exact task-breadcrumb errors. Project the updated guidance to `.github/skills/dude-lint/SKILL.md` through build-dev.

Update the capture explanation in `README.md`, the opening lifecycle explanation in `docs/workflow.md`, Definition Rules in `docs/reference.md`, and the brainstorm/define sections in `docs/commands.md`. Replace wording that implies globally unique or globally reserved numbers. Cross-reference the detailed contract rather than copying it throughout the docs.

Do not rewrite historical ledgers, definition evidence, memory entries, or unrelated fixture identities to make the new rule appear older than it is.
