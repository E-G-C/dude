Now cockpit — Clearline variant

Three read-only side-panel states at 380 px, shown twice: once with Clearline's default light roles and once with the documented [data-cl-theme="dark"] variant, so both are visible without changing OS settings. The page chrome around the panels also follows prefers-color-scheme. Working controls are Refresh, the source-details disclosure, the chooser rows, and the header's change-feature affordance.

Light theme

Default Clearline roles: --cl-bg, --cl-text, --cl-color-control-border, --cl-color-focus-ring.

Dark theme

Same markup with [data-cl-theme="dark"] on the panel, which remaps --cl-bg, --cl-bg-subtle, --cl-border, --cl-text, --cl-text-muted, --cl-color-control-border, and the focus ring and halo to their documented dark values.
